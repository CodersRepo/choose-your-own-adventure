## 📖 About the Story

You are the commander of the *Helios*, humanity's final hope. With your crewmates **Yusuf Osei** (Engineer) and **Mara Chen** (Biologist), you must navigate 15 years of deep space travel, resource crises, and an alien mystery — all before your sun goes dark.

- 20 story nodes across 4 acts
- 5 endings, including a hidden **True Ending** (First Contact)
- Live crew survival tracking (Yusuf & Mara)
- Hidden cooperation score gates the secret ending

> **Dev mode**: append `?dev=1` to the URL to see your live cooperation score and current page ID as you play.
